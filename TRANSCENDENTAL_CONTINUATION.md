## 0. Status and terminology

**Transcendental continuation** is a project-defined term used by `reimann_scope`.

It is not claimed to be established standard terminology in analytic number theory.

The term names a precise mathematical framework:

> Extend the ordinary analytically continued zeta domain by an additional scale coordinate generated by powers of the transcendental full-turn constant \(\tau=2\pi\), so that the ordinary zeta function is the native \(K=0\) slice and the other \(K\)-states are constructed in parallel across the canonical real algebraic grade domain \(K \in \mathbb A_{\mathbb R} = \overline{\mathbb Q} \cap \mathbb R\) rather than treated as numerical traversals from one state to another.

The usefulness of the framework must be established by exact identities, computation, falsification, and eventually proof.

# 5. Graded arithmetic grids and the incommensurability theorem

For every algebraic grade $K \in \mathbb A_{\mathbb R}$, define the nonzero arithmetic grid:

\[
\boxed{
L_K
=
\{n\tau^K : n\in\pm\mathbb N\}
=
\{n\tau^K : n\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}\}.
}
\]

Each grid $L_K$ is countably infinite: $|L_K|=\aleph_0$. The map $n\mapsto\tau^K n$ is a bijection from $\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$ to $L_K$.

The total TC graded arithmetic universe is:

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak L_\tau
=
\bigcup_{K\in\mathbb A_{\mathbb R}}L_K
=
\{n\tau^K : n\in\pm\mathbb N,\; K\in\mathbb A_{\mathbb R}\}.
}
\]

### Theorem (Rational-Difference Incommensurability — Proved)
For any two algebraic grades $J \ne K \in \mathbb A_{\mathbb R}$ whose difference is rational ($K - J \in \mathbb Q \setminus \{0\}$):

\[
\boxed{
L_J\cap L_K=\emptyset.
}
\]

**Proof**:
Suppose there exist $m, n \in \pm\mathbb N$ such that $m\tau^K = n\tau^J$. Then:
\[
\tau^{K-J} = \frac{n}{m} \in \mathbb Q.
\]
Writing the nonzero rational difference as $K - J = p/q$ with $p \in \mathbb Z \setminus \{0\}$ and $q \in \mathbb N$, raising to the $q$-th power yields:
\[
\tau^p = \left(\frac{n}{m}\right)^q \in \mathbb Q.
\]
Since $p \ne 0 \in \mathbb Z$, $\tau = 2\pi$ would be a root of the non-trivial polynomial $m^q x^{|p|} - n^q = 0$ (or $n^q x^{|p|} - m^q = 0$), implying that $\tau$ is algebraic. This directly contradicts the transcendence of $\tau = 2\pi$ (Lindemann 1882). Thus, no such nonzero integers $m, n$ exist. $\blacksquare$

### Status of Irrational Algebraic Differences: `OPEN_TAU_ALGEBRAIC_EXPONENT_ARITHMETIC`
For $K - J \in \mathbb A_{\mathbb R} \setminus \mathbb Q$ (irrational algebraic), the question whether $\tau^{K-J} \in \mathbb Q$ is **strictly OPEN**.
- The Gelfond–Schneider theorem requires an **algebraic base** ($a^\beta$ is transcendental for algebraic $a \ne 0, 1$ and irrational algebraic $\beta$), and therefore does not apply to the transcendental base $\tau = 2\pi$.
- **Control Counterexample**: Let $b = 2^{1/\sqrt{2}}$. By Gelfond–Schneider, $b$ is transcendental (base 2 algebraic, exponent $1/\sqrt{2}$ irrational algebraic). However:
  \[
  b^{\sqrt{2}} = \left(2^{1/\sqrt{2}}\right)^{\sqrt{2}} = 2^1 = 2 \in \mathbb Q.
  \]
  Thus, $1 \cdot b^{\sqrt{2}} = 2 \cdot b^0$ is an exact integer collision between grades $\sqrt{2}$ and $0$ for base $b$.
- This counterexample proves that **transcendence of a base alone does NOT prevent collision at irrational algebraic exponents**. Full algebraic-grade grid disjointness cannot be asserted for $\tau$ without a dedicated number-theoretic theorem specifically applicable to $2\pi$, and must not be used as an assumed premise downstream.

- **Layer A (Informational Equivalence)**: For each individual grade $K$, $\mathcal Z_\tau(\tau^K s, K) = \zeta(s)$. No new information has been added to $\zeta$.

# 6.2 The Pure Pullback Coherence Baseline (The Null Model Theorem)

To prevent mistaking ordinary coordinate transport for an RH mechanism, we establish what holds for **any** function independently of zeta.

### Theorem (Pure Pullback Null Model)
Let $f: \mathbb C \to \mathbb C$ be an arbitrary meromorphic function, and for each $K \in \mathbb A_{\mathbb R}$ define $F_K(s) = f(\tau^{-K} s)$. Then:
1. **Pullback Invariance**: $F_K(\tau^K z) = f(z)$ for all $z \in \mathbb C$;
2. **Zero Transport**: $f(\rho) = 0 \iff F_K(\tau^K \rho) = 0$;
3. **Derivative Transport**: $\partial_s^m F_K(s) = \tau^{-mK} f^{(m)}(\tau^{-K} s)$;
4. **Radial Invariance**: For any $\rho = 1/2 + \delta + i\gamma$, the normalized radial coordinate $R_\tau(\tau^K \rho, K) = \tau^{-K} \operatorname{Re}(\tau^K \rho) - 1/2 = \delta$ is identically constant across all $K \in \mathbb A_{\mathbb R}$.

**Corollary**: Pure TC pullback imposes **zero radial rigidity** on the roots of a generic function. A hypothetical off-critical zero $\delta \ne 0$ transports through all algebraic grades with complete mathematical consistency. Any constraint excluding $\delta \ne 0$ must arise from coupling between zeta's specific arithmetic/analytic structure (Euler product, primes, functional equation) and the graded grids $L_K$.
