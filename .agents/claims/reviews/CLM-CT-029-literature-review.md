## Authoritative Literature Audit

1. **Weil's Explicit Formula & Positivity Equivalence**:
   - **Weil (1952)**, *Sur les "formules explicites" de la théorie des nombres premiers*, Comm. Sém. Math. Univ. Lund.
   - Proves that the non-negativity of the explicit formula distribution $W(f * f^*) \ge 0$ for all admissible test functions $f$ is equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis. Any spectral route that derives non-negativity without an independent arithmetic computation is equivalent to RH.

2. **Explicit Formulas & Zero Density**:
   - **Titchmarsh (1986)**, *The Theory of the Riemann Zeta-Function*, 2nd ed., Oxford University Press, Chapters IX & XIV.
   - Spectral sums over nontrivial zeros $\sum_\rho w_\rho \delta_\rho^2$ are dual to arithmetic prime sums. The non-vanishing of off-line zero defects is equivalent across all regularized spectral representations.

**Literature Audit Outcome**: The equivalence of spectral zero sets and their dependence on the arithmetic explicit formula are well-established in analytic number theory.
