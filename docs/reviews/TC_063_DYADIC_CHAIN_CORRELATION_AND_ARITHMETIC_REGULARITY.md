## 2. Endpoint Contradiction, Premise Chain, TC Compatibility, and Window-Base Scope

### 2.1 Holomorphic Ratio Contradiction vs. Meromorphic Continuation and Trivial Zeros

For complex coordinates $z = x + i y$ ($x, y \in \mathbb{R}$), Euler's product on $x > 1$ gives the Dirichlet identity
$$L_\lambda(x + i y) := \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\lambda(n)}{n^{x + i y}} = \frac{\zeta(2x + 2 i y)}{\zeta(x + i y)} \qquad (x > 1).$$
Suppose independent arithmetic estimates establish a **holomorphic** (pole-free) continuation $F_{\mathrm{hol}}(z)$ of $L_\lambda(z)$ to the half-plane $\{z = x + i y : x > 1/2\}$. By analytic continuation on the connected domain $\{x > 1/2\} \setminus \{1\}$, $\zeta(z) F_{\mathrm{hol}}(z) = \zeta(2z)$ holds for every $z = x + i y$ with $x > 1/2$ and $z \ne 1$:

- **Right half of critical strip ($1/2 < x < 1$)**: If $\rho = x + i y$ satisfies $1/2 < x < 1$ and $\zeta(\rho) = 0$, then $2\rho = 2x + 2 i y$ has real part $2x > 1$, where Euler's product guarantees $\zeta(2\rho) \ne 0$. Evaluating the holomorphic product identity at $\rho$ gives
  $$0 = \zeta(\rho) F_{\mathrm{hol}}(\rho) = \zeta(2\rho) \ne 0,$$
  an immediate contradiction.
- **Left half of critical strip ($0 < x < 1/2$)**: By `completedRiemannZeta` functional-equation symmetry $\Lambda_\zeta(1 - z) = \Lambda_\zeta(z)$ and nonvanishing of the Gamma/exponential prefactor inside $0 < x < 1$, any zero $\rho = x + i y$ with $0 < x < 1/2$ produces the reflected zero $1 - \rho = (1 - x) + i(-y)$ with $1/2 < 1 - x < 1$, which is impossible by the right-half argument. Hence $x = 1/2$.
- **Why meromorphic continuation alone proves nothing**: $\zeta(2z)/\zeta(z)$ already has an unconditional meromorphic continuation to $\mathbb{C}$, because at any hypothetical zero $\rho$ of $\zeta(z)$ with $\zeta(2\rho) \ne 0$, the meromorphic quotient simply has a compensating pole so $\zeta(z) \cdot (\zeta(2z)/\zeta(z)) \to \zeta(2\rho)$ without contradiction. Only **pole-free holomorphy** of $F_{\mathrm{hol}}$ at $\rho$ forces $\zeta(\rho) F_{\mathrm{hol}}(\rho) = 0$.
- **Why trivial zeros $z = -2n$ ($n \ge 1$) do not contradict the identity**: At $z = -2n$ ($n \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 1}$), the doubled point $2z = -4n$ is also a negative even integer, so $\zeta(-2n) = 0$ and $\zeta(-4n) = 0$ vanish simultaneously; the nonvanishing step $\zeta(2z) \ne 0$ uses $2x > 1$ and does not apply at $-2n$.

The four formal conjuncts — (1) the $x > 1$ Dirichlet ratio & Euler nonvanishing, (2) trivial-zero simultaneous vanishing at $-2(n+1)$, (3) left-half-strip `completedRiemannZeta` reflection, and (4) the right-half-strip pole contradiction from holomorphic $F_{\mathrm{hol}}$ — are proved unconditionally in Lean 4 on `Mathlib`'s actual `riemannZeta` and `completedRiemannZeta` in [`liouville_dirichlet_ratio_pole_contradiction_and_reflection_tc063`](../../formal/RiemannScope/DyadicChainCorrelationRegularity.lean#L390-L512) (with the Dirichlet, trivial-zero, and reflection identities cross-checked in 256-bit `Acb` ball arithmetic in Section 1 of [`data/tc_dyadic_chain_correlation_regularity.json`](../../data/tc_dyadic_chain_correlation_regularity.json), while the compensating-pole remark for meromorphic continuation is a direct analytical observation).

### 2.3 TC Compatibility Principle and Window-Base Scope ($b > 1$ vs. $\tau$)

- **TC Correspondence Without Overclaiming Exact Grade Invariance**: In the Transcendental Continuation (`TC`) programme, admissible descriptions across scales concern **one common arithmetic object** — here the Liouville-weighted Gaussian dilation observable $Q_{\lambda, \infty}(X) = \sum_{n \ge 1} \lambda(n) \varphi_n(X)$, whose Mellin transform represents $L_\lambda(z) = \zeta(2z)/\zeta(z)$. The **arithmetic compatibility law** is the coupled prime-dilation contraction linking scale $Y$ to shifted scales $Y / p^k$ via Selberg's second-order identity and the 2-adic dyadic-chain decomposition (`TC-063` Theorem 3 absorbs the entire same-odd-part chain interaction $S_{\mathrm{same}}(Y)$ unconditionally into $O(1 + \log Y)$). The **constrained quantity** is the logarithmic window energy $E_\infty(Y) = \int_Y^{\tau Y} |Q_{\lambda, \infty}(X)|^2 \frac{dX}{X}$: if `TC063-PREM-01` holds, the compatibility law forces the subpower regularity constraint $\forall \varepsilon > 0,\; E_\infty(Y) \ll_\varepsilon Y^\varepsilon$, which forces $M_Q(w)$ (and hence $\zeta(2z)/\zeta(z)$) to be pole-free on $x > 1/2$, directly contradicting the pole created by any off-critical zero $\rho = x_0 + i y_0$ ($x_0 \ne 1/2$). Crucially, establishing a subpower energy growth bound via Route D **does not** construct the original zero-independent exact grade-invariant functional (`TC-050` established that a faithful positive spectral realization requires exact grade invariance, which an asymptotic growth bound neither proves nor requires).
- **Window Base $b > 1$ vs. $\tau$**: The odd-part bijection, shifted-station disjointness, finite Hilbert inequality, and uniform $O_b(1 + \log Y)$ same-chain bound hold for **every** real multiplicative window base $b > 1$ on $[Y, b Y]$ (with $C_g(b) = 186(b - 1)$ and $C_\psi(b)$ scaling smoothly with $b - 1$ and $\log b$; certified in Section 1 of [`data/tc_dyadic_chain_correlation_regularity.json`](../../data/tc_dyadic_chain_correlation_regularity.json) for $b \in \{2, 3, \tau\}$). ## 4. Exact Odd-Part Decomposition (`Theorem 2`)

Every anchor $m \in I_2(T)$ has an even $2$-adic valuation $v_2(m) = 2j$ ($j \ge 0$), hence a unique representation $m = 4^j u$ with $u \ge 1$ odd. Defining ([`oddChainParts`](../../formal/RiemannScope/DyadicChainCorrelationRegularity.lean#L37-L38), [`dyadicChainExponents`](../../formal/RiemannScope/DyadicChainCorrelationRegularity.lean#L41-L42), [`oddChainGroupedKernel`](../../formal/RiemannScope/DyadicChainCorrelationRegularity.lean#L57-L61), [`sameOddChainSumS`](../../formal/RiemannScope/DyadicChainCorrelationRegularity.lean#L64-L68), [`differentOddChainSumS`](../../formal/RiemannScope/DyadicChainCorrelationRegularity.lean#L75-L79)):
$$O(T) = \{u \ge 1 : u \text{ odd and } 2u \le T\}, \qquad J_u(T) = \{j \ge 0 : 2 \cdot 4^j u \le T\},$$
$$\mathcal{K}_Y(u, v) = \sum_{j \in J_u(T)} \sum_{k \in J_v(T)} \Delta\mathcal{H}_Y(4^j u, 4^k v),$$
multiplicativity $\lambda(4^j u) = (-1)^{2j}\lambda(u) = \lambda(u)$ yields the exact identity on the complete kernel:
$$\Sigma_{\mathrm{off}}(Y; 2) = S_{\mathrm{same}}(Y) + S_{\mathrm{different}}(Y),$$
where
$$S_{\mathrm{same}}(Y) = \sum_{u \in O(T)} \sum_{\substack{j, k \in J_u(T) \\ j \ne k}} \Delta\mathcal{H}_Y(4^j u, 4^k u), \qquad S_{\mathrm{different}}(Y) = \sum_{\substack{u, v \in O(T) \\ u \ne v}} \lambda(u)\,\lambda(v)\,\mathcal{K}_Y(u, v).$$
