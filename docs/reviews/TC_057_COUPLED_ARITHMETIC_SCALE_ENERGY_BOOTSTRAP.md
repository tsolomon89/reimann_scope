## 2. Monotone Scale Envelope $F(Y)$ and the Conditional Polylogarithmic Bootstrap

### Proposition 2.1 (Unconditional Local Boundedness of $F(Y)$ Across Cutoff Jumps)

Define the monotone scale envelope
$$F(Y) := \sup_{1 \le Z \le Y} E(Z), \qquad Y \ge 1.$$
Although $Z \mapsto T(Z) = \lceil Z^{5/4} \rceil$ jumps at every $Z_m = m^{4/5}$ ($m \ge 2$), $E_L(Z)$ satisfies an $L$-uniform linear upper bound for all $L \in \mathbb{N}_0$ and all $Z > 0$:
$$0 \le E_L(Z) \le M_{\mathrm{lin}} Z, \qquad M_{\mathbf{1}} := \frac{1}{2} + 2\left(\frac{3}{2 e}\right)^{3/2}, \qquad M_{\mathrm{lin}} := (\tau - 1) M_{\mathbf{1}}^2,$$
and therefore $0 \le E(Z) \le F(Y) \le M_{\mathrm{lin}} Y < \infty$ for all $1 \le Z \le Y$, and $F$ is nondecreasing on $[1, \infty)$.

*Proof*. For $X > 0$, $|Q_L(X)| \le \sum_{n=1}^\infty \varphi_n(X) = X^{5/2} \sum_{n=1}^\infty g_X(n)$, where $g_X(t) := t^{-3} e^{-X^2/t^2}$ ($t > 0$). Differentiating $\log g_X(t) = -3\log t - X^2 t^{-2}$ gives $\frac{g_X'(t)}{g_X(t)} = \frac{2 X^2 - 3 t^2}{t^3}$, so $g_X$ is strictly increasing on $(0, \sqrt{2/3}\,X]$ and strictly decreasing on $[\sqrt{2/3}\,X, \infty)$, with global maximum $g_X(\sqrt{2/3}\,X) = \left(\frac{3}{2e}\right)^{3/2} X^{-3}$ and total integral $\int_0^\infty t^{-3} e^{-X^2/t^2}\,dt = \frac{1}{2 X^2}$ (via $v = X^2/t^2$). By unimodality on $(0, \infty)$, omitting at most the two integers flanking $\sqrt{2/3}\,X$ from integral comparison yields, for every $X \ge 1$,
$$X^{-1/2} |Q_L(X)| \le X^2 \left(\frac{1}{2 X^2} + 2\left(\frac{3}{2 e}\right)^{3/2} X^{-3}\right) = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{2}{X}\left(\frac{3}{2 e}\right)^{3/2} \le M_{\mathbf{1}},$$
while for $0 < X < 1$, $X^{-1/2}|Q_L(X)| \le X^2 \sum_{n=1}^\infty n^{-3} = \zeta(3) X^2 < \zeta(3) < M_{\mathbf{1}}$. Squaring and integrating over $[Z, \tau Z]$ with respect to $\frac{dX}{X}$ gives $E_L(Z) \le M_{\mathbf{1}}^2 \int_Z^{\tau Z} X\,\frac{dX}{X} = (\tau - 1) M_{\mathbf{1}}^2 Z = M_{\mathrm{lin}} Z$. Taking the supremum of the bounded set $\{E(Z) : 1 \le Z \le Y\} \subseteq [0, M_{\mathrm{lin}} Y]$ establishes finiteness and monotonicity of $F(Y)$ without continuity of $Z \mapsto E(Z)$ (formalized unconditionally in [`abstract_polylog_scale_bootstrap_and_weighted_contraction_tc057`](file:///c:/Development/Projects/reimann_scope/formal/RiemannScope/LiouvilleSquareDilation.lean) parts (a)–(b)). $\square$

### Theorem 2.3 (Abstract Polylogarithmic Scale Bootstrap — `CONDITIONAL_ON_RECURSION`, Lean Theorem 21)

*Scope Label*: **`CONDITIONAL_ON_RECURSION`**. Suppose that for every $\eta > 0$ there exist $Y\)-independent constants $A_\eta, B_\eta \ge 0$, $c_\eta > 0$, and $Y_\eta \ge e^4$ such that for all $Y \ge Y_\eta$, $2 \le U_\eta(Y) \le Y$ with $U_\eta(Y) \ge c_\eta (\log Y \log\log Y)^2$ and
$$F(Y) \le A_\eta Y^\eta + B_\eta F\!\left(\frac{Y}{U_\eta(Y)}\right).$$
Then **for every $\varepsilon > 0$** (without requiring $B_\eta < 1$), there exists $C_\varepsilon > 0$ such that $E(Y) \le F(Y) \le C_\varepsilon Y^\varepsilon$ for all $Y \ge 1$.

*Proof*. Fix $\varepsilon > 0$ and set $\eta := \varepsilon / 2 \in (0, \varepsilon)$. Since $U_\eta(Y) \to \infty$ as $Y \to \infty$, choose $Y_*(\varepsilon) \ge \max(Y_\eta, 1)$ large enough that $B_\eta U_\eta(Y)^{-\varepsilon} \le \frac{1}{2}$ for all $Y \ge Y_*(\varepsilon)$. Define the compact-base constant
$$C_\varepsilon := \max\!\left(F(Y_*(\varepsilon)),\; 2 A_\eta Y_*(\varepsilon)^{\eta - \varepsilon}\right) \ge 0.$$
Notice $Y \ge Y_*(\varepsilon)$ and $\eta < \varepsilon$ imply $A_\eta Y^{\eta - \varepsilon} \le A_\eta Y_*(\varepsilon)^{\eta - \varepsilon} \le \frac{1}{2} C_\varepsilon$. We prove $F(Y) \le C_\varepsilon Y^\varepsilon$ for all $Y \ge 1$ by strong induction on the dyadic height $m(Y) := \lfloor \log_2 Y \rfloor \in \mathbb{N}_0$:
- If $1 \le Y \le Y_*(\varepsilon)$ (which includes the entire base $m(Y) \le \lfloor \log_2 Y_*(\varepsilon) \rfloor$, and in particular $1 \le Y < 2$), monotonicity of $F$ and $Y^\varepsilon \ge 1$ yield $F(Y) \le F(Y_*(\varepsilon)) \le C_\varepsilon \le C_\varepsilon Y^\varepsilon$.

### Proposition 2.4 (Weighted Multi-Scale Contraction Criterion & $(\log Y)^b$ Exponent Barrier)

1. **Weighted Multi-Scale Contraction**: For a finite multi-scale inequality $F(Y) \le \mathcal{E}(Y) + \sum_{d \in \mathcal{D}(Y)} w_d(Y) F(Y/d)$ ($w_d(Y) \ge 0$, $2 \le d \le Y$), the exact homogeneous contraction factor at exponent $\sigma > 0$ is
   $$\Theta_\sigma(Y) := \sum_{d \in \mathcal{D}(Y)} w_d(Y) d^{-\sigma}.$$
   Whenever $\Theta_\sigma(Y) \le \theta < 1$ and $\mathcal{E}(Y) \le A Y^\eta$ ($\eta < \sigma$) for $Y \ge Y_*$, height induction yields $F(Y) \ll_\sigma Y^\sigma$ with $C_\sigma = \max\!\left(F(Y_*), \frac{A Y_*^{\eta - \sigma}}{1 - \theta}\right)$ (Lean Theorem 21 part (e)).
2. **Polylogarithmic Coefficient-Mass Penalty Barrier**: Never absorb $Y$-dependent factors into $B_\eta$. If $\sum_{d \ge U(Y)} w_d(Y) = B_0 (\log Y)^b$ ($b > 0$) while $U(Y) = c (\log Y)^a$ ($a > 0$), then terms near $d \asymp U(Y)$ yield
   $$B_0 (\log Y)^b U(Y)^{-\sigma} = B_0 c^{-\sigma} (\log Y)^{b - a \sigma}.$$
   For $\sigma \le b / a$, $(\log Y)^{b - a\sigma} \not\to 0$ (diverging to $+\infty$ for $\sigma < b/a$), blocking any all-$\varepsilon$ conclusion below the critical exponent $\sigma_{\mathrm{crit}} = b / a$.

## 3. Route A: Compensated Möbius-Rough Prime-Filtration Descent

### Theorem 3.1 (General Möbius-Rough Inclusion-Exclusion Descent, Lean Theorem 22)

Let $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ be completely multiplicative with $a(1) = 1$. For every integer $U \ge 2$, integer $T \ge 0$, and real $X \ge 0$, let $\mathcal{P}_{[U,T]} := \{p \text{ prime} : U \le p \le T\}$, $N_{[U,T]} := \prod_{p \in \mathcal{P}_{[U,T]}} p$, and $\mathcal{R}_{[U,T]} := \{d \in \mathbb{N} : U \le d \le T,\; d \mid N_{[U,T]}\}$ (the non-unit squarefree $[U,T]$-rough integers $\le T$). Then
$$Q_{a,T}(X) = S_{a,<U,T}(X) - \sum_{d \in \mathcal{R}_{[U,T]}} \frac{\mu(d)\,a(d)}{\sqrt{d}} \, Q_{a,\lfloor T/d \rfloor}\!\left(\frac{X}{d}\right).$$

*Proof*. By Möbius inversion $\sum_{d \mid \gcd(n, N_{[U,T]})} \mu(d) = \mathbf{1}_{\gcd(n, N_{[U,T]}) = 1}$, an integer $1 \le n \le T$ has $\gcd(n, N_{[U,T]}) = 1$ if and only if every prime factor $p \mid n$ satisfies $p < U$. Separating $d = 1$ from $d \ge 2$ (noting $d \mid N_{[U,T]}$ and $d \ge 2$ imply $d \ge U$, while $d \mid n \le T$ implies $d \le T$ and $q := n/d \le \lfloor T/d \rfloor$), substituting $a(d q) = a(d) a(q)$, and invoking exact kernel scaling $\varphi_{d q}(X) = d^{-1/2} \varphi_q(X/d)$ yields
$$S_{a,<U,T}(X) = \sum_{n=1}^T a(n) \varphi_n(X) \sum_{d \mid \gcd(n, N_{[U,T]})} \mu(d) = Q_{a,T}(X) + \sum_{d \in \mathcal{R}_{[U,T]}} \frac{\mu(d)\,a(d)}{\sqrt{d}} \, Q_{a,\lfloor T/d \rfloor}\!\left(\frac{X}{d}\right).$$
- **Uniform Liouville Sign Feature**: Because $\lambda(p) = -1$ at **every** prime $p$, every squarefree $d = p_1 \cdots p_r \in \mathcal{R}_{[U,T]}$ satisfies $\mu(d)\lambda(d) = (-1)^r (-1)^r = +1$, regardless of $\omega(d) = r$:

## 4. Routes B & C: Logarithmic Prime-Power Descent, Kernel-Shift Non-Closure, and Second-Order Selberg Cancellation

### Theorem 4.1 (Exact Finite Logarithmic Prime-Power Descent & Shift Hierarchy, Lean Theorem 23)

Let $a : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}$ be completely multiplicative with $a(1) = 1$. For every integer $T \ge 0$, shift order $m \in \mathbb{N}_0$, and real $X > 0$:
$$\Sigma_{a,\mathrm{pp}}^{[m]}(X; T) := \sum_{n=1}^T a(n) (\log n) \left(\log\frac{X}{n}\right)^m \varphi_n(X) = \sum_{\substack{p^k \le T \\ p \text{ prime},\, k \ge 1}} a(p)^k (\log p)\, p^{-k/2}\, Q_{a,\lfloor T/p^k \rfloor}^{[m]}\!\left(\frac{X}{p^k}\right).$$
In particular, for $a = \lambda$ ($\lambda(p)^k = (-1)^k$ at every prime $p$) and $m = 0$:
$$(\log X)\, Q_{\lambda,T}(X) = \sum_{\substack{p^k \le T \\ p \text{ prime},\, k \ge 1}} (-1)^k (\log p)\, p^{-k/2}\, Q_{\lambda,\lfloor T/p^k \rfloor}\!\left(\frac{X}{p^k}\right) + Q_{\lambda,T}^{[1]}(X),$$
and iterating $M \ge 1$ times yields the exact finite telescope
$$Q_{\lambda,T}(X) = \sum_{m=0}^{M-1} \frac{\Sigma_{\lambda,\mathrm{pp}}^{[m]}(X; T)}{(\log X)^{m+1}} + \frac{Q_{\lambda,T}^{[M]}(X)}{(\log X)^M} \qquad (X > 1).$$

*Proof*. For $T \in \{0, 1\}$, both sides vanish identically ($\log 1 = 0$ and $\{p^k \le T\} = \emptyset$). For $1 \le n \le T$, $\log n = \sum_{p^k \mid n} \log p$ (Mangoldt identity). Exchanging the finite sums over $1 \le n \le T$ and $p^k \le T$ via $n = p^k q$ ($1 \le q \le \lfloor T/p^k \rfloor$) uses $a(p^k q) = a(p)^k a(q)$, $\varphi_{p^k q}(X) = p^{-k/2}\varphi_q(X/p^k)$, and $\log\!\left(\frac{X}{p^k q}\right) = \log\!\left(\frac{X/p^k}{q}\right)$, leaving the exact shift exponent $m$ invariant inside $Q_{a,\lfloor T/p^k \rfloor}^{[m]}(X/p^k)$. Writing $\log n = \log X - \log(X/n)$ gives $(\log X) Q_{a,T}^{[m]}(X) = \Sigma_{a,\mathrm{pp}}^{[m]}(X; T) + Q_{a,T}^{[m+1]}(X)$, and induction on $M$ gives the $M$-step telescope (machine-checked in [`logarithmic_prime_power_descent_and_kernel_shift_tc057`](file:///c:/Development/Projects/reimann_scope/formal/RiemannScope/LiouvilleSquareDilation.lean)). $\square$

### Theorem 5.1 (Exact Cutoff Comparison & Explicit Rational Counterexample, Lean Theorem 25)

### Proposition 5.2 (Linear vs. Quadratic Aggregate Cutoff Replacement Error)

Individual $O(1)$ cofactor cutoff errors do **not** sum to $O(1)$ linearly across primes:
- **Linear Prime-Sum Replacement**: Replacing $Q_{L_p}(X/p)$ by $Q_{T(Y/p)}(X/p)$ *inside* $\sum_{U \le p \le Y} p^{-1/2} Q_{L_p}(X/p)$ before squaring commits pointwise error $\Delta_p(X) = \sum_{T(Y/p) < n \le L_p} \lambda(n)\varphi_n(X/p)$ of norm $\|\Delta_p\|_{L^2([Y,\tau Y], dX/X)} = O(1)$ on $p \in [Y/4, Y/2]$ (where $T(Y/p)$ and $L_p$ differ by $\asymp p^{1/4}$).
