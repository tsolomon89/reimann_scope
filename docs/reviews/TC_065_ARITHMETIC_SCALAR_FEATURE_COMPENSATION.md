### 2.2 Shared Arithmetic Mechanism Behind $U_Y$ and $V_Y$ (Proved in Lean Theorem 2)

Every station in $[1, T] \cap \mathbb{N}$ splits disjointly as $[1, T] = \Omega_2(T) \sqcup \Phi_2(T)$, where
$$\Omega_2(T) = \{1 \le n \le T : \operatorname{Odd}(v_2(n)) \lor 2n \le T\}, \qquad \Phi_2(T) = \{1 \le n \le T : \operatorname{Even}(v_2(n)) \land T < 2n\}.$$
In [Theorem 2](../../formal/RiemannScope/ScalarFeatureCompensation.lean#L1443-L1626) (`paired_station_partition_divisor_synthesis_and_telescoping_tc065`) we prove unconditionally:

1. **Exact Liouville balance and odd channel cutoff**: $\sum_{n \in \Omega_2(T)} \lambda(n) = 0$ and $\sum_{n \in \Phi_2(T)} \lambda(n) = L(T)$; for every odd prime power $p^k$ with $2 p^k > T$, every multiple of $p^k$ in $[1, T]$ is $p^k$ itself, which has $v_2(p^k) = 0$ (even) and $2 p^k > T$, hence $p^k \in \Phi_2(T)$ and $V_{Y,(p,k)} \equiv 0$. Consequently, the active paired feature channels are restricted to $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{act}}(T) = \{(p, k) : p^k \le T \land (p = 2 \lor 2 p^k \le T)\}$.
2. **Complete-observable divisor synthesis**:
   $$U_Y(X) = 2 P_{\Omega_2}(X) - O_{\Omega_2}(X) = Q_T(X) + A_T(X) - S_{\mathrm{spill}, T}(X) - \Phi_{\mathrm{fringe}, Y}^{\mathrm{scal}}(X), \qquad P_{\Omega_2}(X) = \sum_{(p,k) \in \mathcal{P}(T)} V_{Y,(p,k)}(X),$$
   where $A_T(X) = -\sum_{n=1}^{2T} \beta_Y(n)\varphi_n(X)/(\log X)^2$ denotes the known compensated profile (strictly distinct from the core scalar diagonal $J_A(Y) = \sum_{a \in C(T)}\|\mathcal{A}_a\|_Y^2$), $V_{Y,(p,k)}(X) = G_{T,(p,k)}(X) - G_{\Phi_2,(p,k)}(X)$, and every full prime-power channel satisfies the exact shifted-dilation law
   $$G_{T,(p,k)}(X) = (-1)^k \frac{\log p}{p^{k/2}\log X}\, Q_{\lfloor T/p^k \rfloor}\!\left(\frac{X}{p^k}\right).$$
3. **Pointwise telescoping of $s_Y(n)$**: On $1 \le n \le T$, the smooth open-core coefficient satisfies $s_Y(n) = \sum_{e \mid n,\, D_0 < e \le T-1} \lambda(n/e)(R_{\mathrm{osc}}(e) - R_{\mathrm{osc}}(e-1)) + \mathbf{1}_{D_0 < T-1} \lambda(n/(D_0+1)) R_{\mathrm{osc}}(D_0)\mathbf{1}_{(D_0+1)\mid n} - \mathbf{1}_{D_0 < T-1}\mathbf{1}_{n=T} R_{\mathrm{osc}}(T-1)$ with exact Selberg difference $R_{\mathrm{osc}}(e) - R_{\mathrm{osc}}(e-1) = w_{2,\lambda}(e) - c_\lambda - (M_{\mathrm{sq},2}(e) - M_{\mathrm{sq},2}(e-1))$ and exact absolute Selberg divisor sum $\sum_{e \mid n} \lvert w_{2,\lambda}(e)\rvert = (\log n)^2$.
