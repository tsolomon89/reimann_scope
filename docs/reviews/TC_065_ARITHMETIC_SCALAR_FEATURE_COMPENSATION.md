### 3.1 Supporting Hilbert Budget vs. Arithmetic Realizations

For any linear map $L_Y : \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{feat},Y} \to \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{scal},Y}$ with residual $R_Y := U_Y - 2 L_Y V_Y$, [Theorem 3](../../formal/RiemannScope/ScalarFeatureCompensation.lean#L1878-L2085) (`arithmetic_synthesis_hilbert_budget_operator_norm_and_obstructions_tc065`) formalizes:
