## 4. Controls, Exact Finite-Prime Convolution Invariants, and Attribution Scope

### 4.1 Exact Convolution Formula $(\mathbf{1}*a_S)(n)$, Three-Invariant Separation, and Matched $z = 1$ Laurent Compensation (`CONCRETE_ANALYTIC_IN_LEAN` + `ANALYTIC_PROVED_OUTSIDE_LEAN`)

For any finite prime set $S$ ($S = \emptyset$ giving $a_\emptyset = \mathbf{1}$, $S = \{2\}$ giving $a_{\{2\}}$, and $S = \{3, 5, 7\}$ giving $a_{\{3,5,7\}}$), the completely multiplicative parity sequence $a_S(n) := (-1)^{\sum_{p \in S} v_p(n)}$ has Dirichlet series $D_{a_S}(z) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty a_S(n)n^{-z} = \zeta(z)\prod_{p \in S}\frac{1 - p^{-z}}{1 + p^{-z}}$ on $x > 1$ ($z = x + i y$), which retains a **simple pole** at $z = 1$ (unlike $D_\lambda(z) = \zeta(2z)/\zeta(z)$, which has a simple zero at $z = 1$ because $(\mathbf{1} * \lambda)(n) = \mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{sq}}(n) \in \{0, 1\}$).

1. **Exact Multiplicative Divisor-Convolution Formula (`CONCRETE_ANALYTIC_IN_LEAN` in [`finite_prime_parity_divisor_convolution_formula_tc061r`](file:///c:/Development/Projects/reimann_scope/formal/RiemannScope/MixedArithmeticCovariance.lean#L3234-L3443), `Theorem 6`)**: Since $\mathbf{1} * a_S$ is multiplicative and $(\mathbf{1} * a_S)(p^e) = \sum_{j=0}^e (-1)^j = \frac{1 + (-1)^e}{2} \in \{0, 1\}$ for $p \in S$ whereas $(\mathbf{1} * a_S)(p^e) = \sum_{j=0}^e 1 = e + 1$ for $p \notin S$, for every integer $n \ge 1$:
   $$(\mathbf{1} * a_S)(n) = \prod_{p \in S}\frac{1 + (-1)^{v_p(n)}}{2}\prod_{\substack{p \mid n \\ p \notin S}}(v_p(n) + 1).$$
   In particular, $(\mathbf{1} * a_\emptyset)(n) = d(n)$ (e.g. $(\mathbf{1} * a_\emptyset)(12) = 6 \ne 1$), and $(\mathbf{1} * a_{\{2\}})(n)$ equals $0$ when $v_2(n)$ is odd and $d(n_{\mathrm{odd}})$ when $v_2(n)$ is even (e.g. $(\mathbf{1} * a_{\{2\}})(3) = 2$, $(\mathbf{1} * a_{\{2\}})(6) = 0$, $(\mathbf{1} * a_{\{2\}})(12) = 2$; verified against direct divisor summation for $1 \le n \le 300$ in Section 4).
2. **Strict Separation of the Three Distinct Finite-Prime Control Invariants**:
   - **Divisor-convolution coefficients $(\mathbf{1} * a_S)(n)$**: equal $d(n_{\notin S})$ on $\{n : 2 \mid v_p(n)\ \forall p \in S\}$, hence unbounded on primes outside $S$ (correcting the earlier `TC-061` text that omitted the $\prod_{p \notin S}(v_p(n)+1)$ factor).
   - **Natural density of nonzero support $\{n \ge 1 : (\mathbf{1} * a_S)(n) \ne 0\}$**: equals $\prod_{p \in S}\bigl(1 - \frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{p^2} - \cdots\bigr) = \prod_{p \in S}\frac{p}{p + 1}$ (`1/1` for $S = \emptyset$, `2/3` for $S = \{2\}$, `35/64` for $S = \{3, 5, 7\}$).
   - **Simple-pole residue at $z = 1$ of $D_{a_S}(z)$**: equals $\operatorname*{Res}_{z=1} D_{a_S}(z) = \prod_{p \in S}\frac{1 - p^{-1}}{1 + p^{-1}} = \prod_{p \in S}\frac{p - 1}{p + 1} > 0$ (`1/1` for $S = \emptyset$, `1/3` for $S = \{2\}$, `1/4` for $S = \{3, 5, 7\}$).
3. **Exact Matched Second-Order $z = 1$ Laurent Drift Compensation (`ANALYTIC_PROVED_OUTSIDE_LEAN`)**: Since $\frac{D_{a_S}'(1+u)}{D_{a_S}(1+u)} = -\frac{1}{u} + \beta_S + O(u)$ as $u \to 0$ with $\beta_S := \gamma_E + \sum_{p \in S}\frac{2 p\log p}{p^2 - 1}$, $\frac{D_{a_S}''(z)}{D_{a_S}(z)} = \frac{2}{(z-1)^2} - \frac{2\beta_S}{z-1} + O(1)$ has a **double pole** at $z = 1$, giving exact two-parameter Perron drift compensation
   $$M_{2,a_S}(d) := 2 d\log d - \left(2 + 2\gamma_E + 4\sum_{p \in S}\frac{p\log p}{p^2 - 1}\right)d = 2 d\log d - (2 + 2\beta_S)d, \qquad R_{a_S}(d) := W_{2,a_S}(d) - M_{2,a_S}(d),$$

### 5.2 Complete-Core Adjusted Expansion ($\beta_Y$ vs $s_Y$) and Compact $(0, 2, 4)$ Signed Gram Representation (`CONCRETE_ANALYTIC_IN_LEAN`)
